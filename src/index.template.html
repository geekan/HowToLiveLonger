How to read the research ↓

Age, smoking and other factors can affect both daily habits and health outcomes.

  • Effect estimates describe differences between groups; intervals express uncertainty. Read them alongside the comparison group, baseline risk and follow-up.
  • All-cause mortality, disease occurrence and biomarkers answer different questions. First check what the study measured.
  • Sources show what was read: metadata, abstract or key full-text sections. Follow the link to continue with the original research.
  • Topics such as food, movement and sleep continue to grow. New studies and corrections are welcome.
